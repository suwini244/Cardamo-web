Our research

Four decisions a cardamom farmer makes by eye

Cardamom is one of Sri Lanka's high-value export spices [16]. Its yield depends on catching leaf and pod problems early, and its price depends on visible quality and on when it is sold. Today most of these calls are made by inspection and experience. Cardamo puts machine learning behind each of them.

Literature survey

What prior work established

Leaf disease detection

  • CNNs can recognise plant diseases from leaf images, shown mostly on large multi-crop datasets [2], [3].
  • Cardamom-specific studies exist [5], [6], but cardamom is under-represented in public datasets and controlled-image models may not hold up on field photos.
  • Grad-CAM can show which region of an image drove a prediction [4].

Pod disease detection

  • The capsule is the part that is sold, yet pod-level threats get less attention than leaf disease in the literature.
  • Capsule borer entry holes are small relative to the pod and thrips leave surface scarring, so field inspection depends on experience.
  • Detection studies usually stop at a label, with no guidance on what the farmer should do next.

Pod quality grading

  • Cardamom has been graded with YOLOv8 [7] and with classical ML on colour, size and surface features [10].
  • Electronic-nose fusion [8] and image-based size measurement [9] perform well but need extra hardware or controlled setups.
  • Few of these approaches are packaged for a farmer's phone.

Market forecasting

  • Sri Lankan commodity prices have been forecast with time-series models [12] and machine learning [15].
  • Currency movement affects export prices [13], and processing costs shape spice-sector margins [14].
  • None of these link a price forecast to the farmer's choice between selling fresh and drying for a later sale.

Research gap

What is missing, and how Cardamo responds
GapIn existing workCardamo's response

Cardamom-specific models

Disease models are trained on other crops or on controlled images. Cardamom leaf and pod data are scarce.

Dedicated cardamom datasets (1,724 leaf and 2,753 pod images) with transfer learning, augmentation and class weighting.

From diagnosis to care

Detection ends at a class label. The farmer still has to work out what to do.

Each pod diagnosis returns a risk level, a farmer action and organic management options.

Consistent, phone-ready grading

Manual grading varies between graders. Automated grading often needs special hardware.

A MobileNetV2 grader that works from an ordinary photo, exports to TensorFlow Lite and issues a quality certificate.

Forecasts tied to farm economics

Price forecasts ignore the conversion ratio, drying, storage and quality-loss costs of drying a harvest.

A four-week dried-price forecast feeds a profit rule that says sell fresh, hold, or dry and store.

Trust and safe inputs

Black-box predictions, and closed-set classifiers that will label any photo they are given.

Confidence thresholds, Grad-CAM heatmaps for leaves, and rejection of photos that are not cardamom.

Research problem

How can machine learning give Sri Lankan cardamom farmers reliable, explainable support for crop health, harvest quality and selling decisions, in one platform that works from a phone?

Each component answers one part of this question with its own dataset, model and evaluation, and all four are served through the same web and mobile application.

Objectives

Main objective

Design, build and evaluate Cardamo, a web and mobile cultivation management system that combines three image-based deep-learning models with a market forecasting model, each packaged for lightweight deployment and validated on independent test data.

Specific objective 1

Leaf disease detection

Classify cardamom leaf photos as Healthy, Leaf Blight or Phyllosticta Leaf Spot with EfficientNetB0, and show the evidence behind each prediction with Grad-CAM.

K. J. M. D. Imasha Specific objective 2

Pod disease detection

Tell capsule borer damage from cardamom thrips damage on pod photos, and turn each diagnosis into practical care guidance.

Dissanayake K.S.S. Specific objective 3

Pod quality grading

Grade harvested pods as Grade A, B or C with at least 90% test accuracy, with confidence-aware results and a quality certificate.

Kulasooriya K.S.D Specific objective 4

Market intelligence

Forecast the dried-cardamom price four weeks ahead and recommend whether to sell fresh, hold, or dry and store.

D. I. Delpechitra

Our solution

One app, four AI services

Farmers use the Cardamo web or mobile app. A shared FastAPI backend checks each upload, screens out photos that are not a cardamom leaf or pod, and sends the request to the right model. All three image models were exported to TensorFlow Lite for lightweight deployment.

Explainable leaf disease classification

Can a transfer-learning model tell healthy cardamom leaves from leaf blight and Phyllosticta leaf spot, and show why it decided?

K. J. M. D. Imasha
  • Healthy
  • Leaf Blight
  • Phyllosticta Leaf Spot
  1. Resize leaf images to 224 × 224 and augment them with flips, small rotations, zoom and contrast changes.
  2. Weight the loss to offset the smaller Leaf Blight class (weight 2.051 against 0.736 for Healthy).
  3. Train an ImageNet-pretrained EfficientNetB0 head with the backbone frozen, then fine-tune about the last 30 layers at a lower learning rate.
  4. Generate Grad-CAM heatmaps to check that the model looks at lesions, not background.
  5. Export to TensorFlow Lite and serve through the web and mobile apps, rejecting non-cardamom leaves.

Component 2 · Pod disease detectionIT22223180

Pod disease detection and caring support

Can a lightweight classifier separate capsule borer from cardamom thrips damage, and hand the farmer a care plan with the result?

Dissanayake K.S.S.
  • capsule_borer
  • cardamom_thrips
  1. Load 224 × 224 pod images in batches of 32 with flip, rotation, zoom and contrast augmentation.
  2. Train a new Global Average Pooling, Dropout (0.35) and two-class Softmax head on a frozen EfficientNetB0 for 15 epochs.
  3. Fine-tune about the last 30 backbone layers with a lower learning rate and keep the best checkpoint.
  4. Convert to Float32 TensorFlow Lite and confirm it returns the same probabilities as the Keras model.
  5. Attach issue, risk level, farmer action and organic options to every prediction, and reject photos that are not pods.

Component 3 · Pod quality gradingIT22158840

AI-based pod quality grading

Can a lightweight image classifier grade cardamom pods into practical quality classes, flag uncertain photos and run inside a mobile-ready app?

Kulasooriya K.S.D
  1. Find the pod against the light background with OpenCV contours, crop it, pad it and place it on a square white canvas (zero crop failures).
  2. Augment with flips, rotation, zoom, contrast and brightness changes, and weight classes by their training counts.
  3. Train a MobileNetV2 head with the backbone frozen, then fine-tune about the last 35 layers at a lower learning rate.
  4. Show a grade only when the top probability is at least 0.70; otherwise ask the farmer to retake the photo.
  5. Export as SavedModel and TensorFlow Lite, and present the result as a quality certificate.

Component 4 · Market intelligenceIT22228758

Market prediction and profit optimisation

Can a four-week price forecast be turned into a clear, auditable answer to the farmer's question: sell fresh now, or dry and store?

D. I. Delpechitra
  1. Build 77 features from weekly prices across six regions and four grades: lags, rolling statistics, season and estimated economic variables.
  2. Split the weeks in time order: train 2018-01-01 to 2023-10-09, validate to 2024-12-30, test 2025-01-06 to 2026-03-23.
  3. Compare six regressors and a baseline on the validation period, then retrain the best on train plus validation.
  4. Estimate fresh revenue against dried net profit after conversion ratio, drying, storage and quality-loss costs.
  5. Serve the forecast and recommendation through FastAPI to the web and mobile apps.
Recommendation rule

Threshold = the larger of 8% of fresh revenue or LKR 3,000. Dried profit above fresh revenue by more than the threshold gives DRY_AND_STORE; below by more than it gives SELL_FRESH; anything in between gives HOLD_MONITOR.
